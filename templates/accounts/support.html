Getting started

How do I plan my first trip?

Go to My trips and choose Plan a trip. Add a destination and your travel dates, and Wunderlust sets up a trip space with a preparation checklist and a flexible day-by-day itinerary.

What’s on my Overview page?

Overview is your home base: upcoming trips, how ready you are for them, upcoming itinerary activity, trips shared with you and today’s travel notices — all in one place.

Can I use Wunderlust on my phone?

Yes. Every page adjusts to smaller screens, so you can check your plans and tick off your checklist on the go.

Trips & itineraries

How do I add activities to a day?

Open a trip from My trips, find the day in the itinerary and add an activity with a time and location. Leave the time empty for something flexible.

What are “Suggested” activities?

When you create a trip with a suggested itinerary, Wunderlust adds ideas for your destination. They’re marked Suggested — keep the ones you like and remove the rest.

Why did some old activities disappear?

To keep things tidy, activities from days more than 30 days in the past are cleared automatically.

Travelling with others

How do I invite someone to my trip?

Open the trip and copy its invite link. Anyone with a Wunderlust account who opens the link can join the trip.

Can people I invite see everything?

Members can see the shared trip, its itinerary and its updates. Only share your invite link with people you’re travelling with.

Where do I find trips shared with me?

Trips you’ve joined appear in My trips alongside your own, and on your Overview page.

Readiness & travel info

What is the Readiness page?

Readiness tracks your travel documents and packing list for upcoming trips, with a progress bar so you can see what’s left to do.

Can I add my own checklist items?

Yes. Open a trip and add custom items to its checklist, including quantities for things you need more than one of.

Where does travel information come from?

Travel information includes notices for your destinations as well as general travel guidance shared by the Wunderlust team.

Notifications & activity

Where are my notifications?

Click the inbox icon at the top of the page. It shows today’s notifications, and the number on it tells you how many are unread.

Where do older notifications go?

Each day, earlier notifications move to Activity in your profile menu, where they’re kept for 30 days. Use the filters there to switch between notifications and your activity log.

What does “Mark read” do?

It clears the unread dot and lowers the count on your inbox icon. The notification itself stays until it moves to Activity.

Your account

How do I fill in my profile?

Open View profile from your profile menu and choose Edit on About me. You can fill in any details that are still empty.

Why can’t I edit some of my details?

Once a detail like your name or email is saved, it’s locked to keep your account consistent. If something needs correcting, contact your Wunderlust administrator.

How do I change my password?

Go to Settings in your profile menu. Enter your current password, then your new one twice.

Why am I still signed in after closing my browser?

If you ticked Remember me when signing in, you stay signed in for 14 days. Use Sign out in your profile menu on shared devices.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings and choose Delete account. You’ll be asked for your password. This permanently removes your account and your trips, and can’t be undone.
