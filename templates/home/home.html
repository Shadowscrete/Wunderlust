Get the app

Put your trip in your pocket.

Wunderlust installs straight from your browser: no app store and almost no storage. It opens full-screen from your home screen and keeps your upcoming trips available offline.

  • Android & Chrome: tap Install Wunderlust above, or open the browser menu and choose Install app.
  • iPhone & iPad: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Desktop: click the install icon at the right of the address bar.

Why we built it

Less chasing,
more travelling.

Every group has one organizer, asking the same questions on repeat: Did you book your flight? Is your passport still valid? What time are we meeting? Wunderlust answers them in one place, so the organizer gets to enjoy the trip too. Plan the essentials together, then leave room for the parts you didn't plan.
